A subscription, or the albums you bought.

Where do I find these?

Log in at play.qobuz.com with DevTools → Network open, find the user/login request and copy user.id and user_auth_token from its response. streamrip login qobuz can also catch them for you.

Deezer

{{ badge('deezer') }}

A subscription. Streamrip needs the cookie of your login.

Where do I find it?

Log in at deezer.com, open DevTools → Application (or Storage) → Cookies and copy the value of the arl cookie.

SoundCloud

{{ badge('soundcloud') }}

Works as a guest. A login gives better quality and higher limits; if it fails, streamrip goes on as a guest.

Where do I find it?

Log in at soundcloud.com, open DevTools → Application (or Storage) → Cookies and copy the value of the oauth_token cookie.

Spotify

{{ badge('spotify') }}

One app and one login for downloads (the track lists and tags; the audio comes from YouTube Music) and for your liked songs and playlists. The app's owner needs Premium.

How do I make the app?
  1. Create an app in the Spotify developer dashboard.
  2. Redirect URI: {{ copy(redirect_uris.spotify) }}
  3. Tick Web API only, save, and copy the Client ID.

Tidal library

{{ badge('tidal-library') }}

Reading and changing your Tidal playlists and favourites goes through Tidal's official API, with a developer app of your own. Downloading does not need it.

How do I make the app?
  1. Create New App in the TIDAL developer dashboard.
  2. Redirect URI: {{ copy(redirect_uris['tidal-library']) }}
  3. Scopes: collection.read, collection.write, playlists.read, playlists.write and user.read.
  4. Copy the Client ID and the Client Secret.

If Tidal says 11102, check the Client ID, the redirect URI and the scopes of your app.
